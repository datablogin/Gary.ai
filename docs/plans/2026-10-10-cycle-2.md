## Research choices to freeze before tests

Freeze window length **180 sessions**, target band **last 20 sessions**, seed **20261010**, calibration end **2024-12-31**, and a full-context chronological purge. Reserve AMGN and SIRI entirely for the locked cross-symbol holdout. The other six sources receive two seeded calibration endpoints and one eligible later holdout endpoint each; the two reserved sources receive three locked endpoints each. This yields 24 random windows. Add admissible frozen-detector enriched events, keep split-intersecting windows out, and report embargo/exclusion/duplicate counts. Expected pilot before duplicate merging: 15 open calibration cards and 13 locked cards; measured generation decides final counts.

Normalize reviewer-visible OHLC consistently to first visible close = 100, and volume to its within-card median. Use relative session offsets, with completion constrained to −19 through 0; source dates remain private. Detector scoring uses exactly the same 180 visible bars. No future bar, ticker, absolute price/volume, prediction, sampling stratum or source identity enters the public card.

Labels distinguish `completed`, `none`, `uncertain`, `invalid`; completed labels can contain several kind/completion-offset events. Preserve a versioned submission/revision trail per reviewer/card. The study registers exactly two distinct reviewers with explicit human/agent roles. A case enters recognition denominators only when both latest labels are certain (`completed` or `none`) and their full sorted `(kind, completion_offset)` sets and decisions agree exactly. Different completion offsets are disagreement, even when within detector matching tolerance. There is no third-reviewer vote or individual-reviewer recognition score this cycle. Disagreement, uncertainty, invalid and pending cases remain unscored. Match consensus events to predictions one-to-one within kind at ±3 sessions, require detector availability at or after independent completion, and report signed timing differences. Summarize strata separately; rates with zero denominators are null/NA. Only calibration cases can be labeled. Do not expose a per-card detector answer after saving.

The complete `summarize_review` result is coordinator-private. The reviewer HTTP projection exposes only progress and the selected reviewer's own label history, with no detector comparison, disagreement result, other reviewers' answers or source/stratum counts. This prevents an aggregate-score oracle during editable labeling. No public score-release feature is built this cycle; freeze complete independent label sets and design release/revision rules in a later cycle before showing recognition comparisons to reviewers.

Exact JSON structures are recorded in [pattern research](../research/2026-10-10-cycle-2-patterns.md) before tests. Do not retroactively move the split to admit a desired hit. These engineering defaults constrain a workflow pilot; Gary's visual ground truth, meaningful recognition accuracy and future holdout release remain unvalidated.
