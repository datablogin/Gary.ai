Do not remove those charges or switch to six-month totals to make Gary's threshold disappear. Headquarters remains **unknown** under this bounded release-only review. Preserve reported SG&A growth and an investigation note separately from a direct headquarters determination.

## Positive and unknown headquarters evidence

Its issuer-hosted Q3 2025 report, PDF page 8, states that the new building opened on **August 25, 2025**, while executive offices remained elsewhere until an expected later move. The separate grand-opening release is published **October 21, 2025**. Those are distinct event/publication dates, not conflicting observations that should be collapsed into one timestamp. [Q3 2025 report](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmc/jpmorgan-chase-and-co/investor-relations/documents/quarterly-earnings/2025/3rd-quarter/corp-q3-2025.pdf), [grand-opening release](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/newsroom/press-releases/2025/grand-opening-270-park).

The positive fact does not settle Gary's lookback. At October 10, 2026, an opening-event-based one-year window would treat August 25 differently from an October 21 publication-based window. Record announced/committed/opened stages and event dates; keep Gary's policy unresolved rather than granting an exemption or silently inventing a year. An evidence record cannot be used before publication even if its underlying event occurred earlier.

Oracle's current office page still labels Austin as world headquarters. This address fact supplies **no negative headquarters-investment clearance** and does not overturn the Nashville commitment evidence retained in the first-cycle report. An unchanged address is not a bounded review of commitments, leases or construction. [Oracle office page](https://www.oracle.com/corporate/contact/field-offices/).

For any proposed `reviewed_no_disqualifying_evidence` state, record the chosen lookback, covered filing/release set, reviewer, actual review timestamp, unresolved ambiguities and expiry. No company in this slice received that state. A keyword search or SG&A below 25% remains insufficient.
