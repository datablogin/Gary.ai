# Strict buy clearance research, 2026-10-12

This note investigates management, headquarters, accounting comparability, and dividend evidence for the website screen. It grants **no fully qualified buy clearance**. A sourced revenue pass or a modest expense increase is only one part of [strategy v1](../strategy-v1.md); classification, current positive trailing P/E below 30, reviewed yield above 2%, complete headquarters evidence, comparable latest-quarter financials, and a completed entry pattern remain required. The user selected only fully qualified buys, so incomplete research candidates cannot fill the Buy List.

The review used primary issuer annual/interim filings, earnings statements, and dividend disclosures retrieved on October 12. Calculations below use current divided by same-quarter prior-year value minus one. Currency is USD; income-statement amounts are millions unless stated otherwise. This is a bounded document review, not evidence that every intervening filing or headquarters project has been examined through the decision date. No headquarters lookback duration is added to Gary's strategy. Missing or inconclusive coverage must remain blocked under [coverage v1](../coverage-schema.md).

## Headquarters evidence and remaining uncertainty

The 25% threshold is an investigation trigger, not a substitute for the headquarters rule. A below-threshold result also cannot prove no new headquarters investment.

- **DHT:** the [2025 20-F](https://ml-eu.globenewswire.com/Resource/Download/b812bbd7-7097-4fc7-8b92-53b32d39cf43) describes leased office space in Monaco, Norway, Singapore, and India. Note 6 reports office right-of-use assets of 5.329 versus 3.952 at year-end 2024; other non-ROU property/equipment is 1.788 versus 0.637. The cover's principal executive address is Clarendon House, whereas the [current contact page](https://www.dhtankers.com/contact/) calls Richmond House the corporate headquarters. This difference does **not** establish a recent move: the [2017 issuer proxy](https://www.dhtankers.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/DHT_Proxy_Statement_May_12_2017.pdf) already references both addresses. Reconcile the locations and current investment evidence before recording clearance.
- **FRO:** the [2025 annual report](https://ml-eu.globenewswire.com/Resource/Download/09634fab-1b1f-450b-969a-5e9078dff8ab) reports office/administrative expenses and related-party office rental arrangements. The [September 16 half-year report](https://ml-eu.globenewswire.com/Resource/Download/458804c1-1f6b-4b8b-9ac6-900fc165b0fc) continues to describe office-space rental and corporate administration; investment disclosure concerns vessels and equipment. These aggregate disclosures do not identify or rule out all headquarters investment.
- **ASC:** [2025 Form 20-F Item 4.D and note 8](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1577437/000110465926024690/asc-20251231x20f.htm) explicitly say there is no material owned property other than vessels and describe leases in Cork, Singapore, and Houston. Office ROU assets increased from 1.159 to 1.780 and office lease liabilities from 1.023 to 1.870. These are useful property disclosures, but an office-lease increase does not establish a new headquarters and does not close the review through October 12.
- **GBCI:** the [Q2 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/868671/000086867126000069/gbci-20260630.htm) reports net premises/equipment additions of 22.611 in the first half versus 12.178 a year earlier. These are aggregate additions, not a headquarters breakdown. Acquisition-related occupancy expense explains part of the expense change; headquarters clearance is still incomplete.
- **CSCO:** [FY2026 Form 10-K Item 2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/858877/000085887726000132/csco-20260725.htm) identifies the existing San Jose headquarters and regional headquarters in Amsterdam and Singapore; it describes existing facilities as suitable. Annual capital expenditure is about 1.4 billion across the business. This supports existing locations, not a definitive no-investment conclusion through October 12.
- **NOG:** the [2025 Form 10-K Office Locations section](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1104485/000110448526000008/nog-20251231.htm) describes 24,641 square feet of leased executive-office space in Minnetonka and says current space is sufficient. The latest quarter's acquisition costs explain a management flag, but do not extend property clearance to the current decision date. Its revenue failure already prevents entry.
- **EOG:** the [2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/821189/000082118926000054/eog-20251231.htm) lists 1111 Bagby, Houston as principal executive offices. Aggregate capex and the headquarters address alone do not rule out an investment. No clearance is recorded.
- **OVV:** [Q2 MD&A](https://investor.ovintiv.com/download/Ovintiv_Q2_2026_10-Q_Final_No_Exhibits.pdf) reports existing Bow office lease costs of 28 versus 26 for the quarter, half recovered through sublease revenue, and workforce restructuring.
