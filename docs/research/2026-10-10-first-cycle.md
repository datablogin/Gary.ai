# First-cycle research synthesis

This is a pilot feasibility investigation, not a complete-exchange coverage audit or validated investment model.

## Pilot verdict

- Price-history access works on the sampled sources/securities: 8/8 chart responses with 1,255 rows each, 8/8 current P/E/dividend responses, and 5/5 operating-company vendor quarterly comparison pairs. Only 1/8 SEC facts/submissions probes succeeded; 0/5 headquarters clearances were validated. Full per-field counts, qualifications and provenance are in [data-coverage.md](data-coverage.md); the coordinator independently inspected the eight raw chart row counts.
- Presence is not freshness: provider-quarter labels can lag new filings. SEC access failures and ambiguity of security classification need visible blockers, not optimistic defaults.
- A deterministic detector is feasible. [Pattern research](pattern-detection.md) proposes delayed causal pivots and inspectable neckline/cup/handle evidence. Inspected third-party candidates are not a ready-made validated solution.
- Pattern geometry, real-chart recognition, and portfolio efficacy are separate gates. No real-chart accuracy or Sharpe improvement has been measured.

## Proposed solution

1. Normalize available-at snapshots and produce explicit field/classification blockers.
2. Build the three pure public interfaces approved by the user, with network-free fixtures and recorded red/green evidence.
3. Expose one local paper-only preview; have an independent agent QA both normal and adversarial cases.
4. Use subsequent cycles to collect blinded real-chart labels, resolve headquarters evidence and data gaps, then widen the universe. Do not silently replace missing facts with inferred positives.

Primary references and actual probe results are linked in the two detailed reports. The proposal is suitable for a synthetic paper demonstration, while real sample entry remains blocked until required evidence passes. Plan reviews are recorded in the linked cycle plan.
